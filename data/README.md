# data/

Derived data behind constitución viva, regenerated by `npm run data`
([`scripts/data/build.ts`](../scripts/data/build.ts)) and validated by
`npm run check:data` against the JSON Schemas in [`schema/`](schema/). Dedicated to the
public domain (CC0 1.0); see [`DATA-LICENSE.md`](../DATA-LICENSE.md). Sources and
retrieval dates: [`docs/fuentes.md`](../docs/fuentes.md). Method and precision:
[`docs/metodo.md`](../docs/metodo.md).

Dates are ISO `YYYY-MM-DD` and, for decrees, the DOF publication date. Article ids are
the article numbers as strings (`"1"` to `"136"`).

| File | Schema | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| `constitution.json` | [`constitution.schema.json`](schema/constitution.schema.json) | Everything the page needs at first load |
| `growth.json` | [`growth.schema.json`](schema/growth.schema.json) | Words per version since 2006, plus the 1917 estimate |
| `text/<id>.json` | [`text.schema.json`](schema/text.schema.json) | Every version of one article since 2006, as diff operations |
| `graph.json` | [`graph.schema.json`](schema/graph.schema.json) | Citation graph of the 36 laws |
| `meta.json` | [`meta.schema.json`](schema/meta.schema.json) | Sources, SHA-256, retrieval dates, cross-checks |
| `graph-sample.json` | | The 60 mentions read by hand and any unresolved article citation |

## constitution.json

- `generated`: build date. `asOf`: DOF date of the latest decree.
- `decrees[]`: `n` (number in the Cámara's chronological list, 1 to 285), `date`, `title`,
  `summary` (the Cámara's, or null), `note`, `url` (published decree, PDF or scanned
  image), `doc`, `process` (legislative process, when linked), `errata[]`, `articles[]`
  (ids of articles 1 to 136 amended, from "Reformas por artículo"; empty when only
  transitory provisions changed), `president` (id), `legislature` (roman numeral).
- `articles[]` (136, in order): `id`, `titulo` and `capitulo` (headings in the current
  text), `reforms[]` (decree numbers, in order), `errata[]`, `words1917` (estimate),
  `words2006`, `wordsNow`, `textVersions` (versions in `text/<id>.json`), `citedBy`
  (law slug to number of citations of this article in the corpus).
- `presidents[]`: `id`, `name`, `short`, `officialName` (as printed by the Cámara),
  `start`, `end`, `decrees[]`, `reportedArticles` (the Cámara's own total for the term,
  which also counts transitory articles).
- `legislatures[]`: `id`, `start`, `end`, `decrees[]`.
- `keyMoments[]`: `n`, `priority` (1 always labelled, 2 when there is room), `es`, `en`.

## growth.json

`coverage` (`from`, `to`, `versions`), `original` (1917 estimate, `estimate: true`) and
`versions[]`: `date`, `commit` (leyes-mx, short), `decrees[]` published since the
previous version, `words` (articles 1 to 136), `added` and `removed` (words, in articles
a decree of the window touched), `editorialAdded` and `editorialRemoved` (articles
changed with no decree in the window), `changed[]` (article ids).

## text/&lt;id&gt;.json

`versions[]` of one article, only when its text changed: `date`, `decrees[]` (decrees of
the window that touched it; empty means an editorial change), `words`, `added`, `removed`
and `ops`. Each op is `[0, n]` (keep the next *n* characters of the previous version),
`[1, "text"]` (insert) or `[-1, "text"]` (delete); the first version is a single insert.
To rebuild the text of version *k*, apply the ops of versions 0 to *k* in order (see
`rebuild()` in [`src/data.ts`](../src/data.ts)). This folder keeps the text of the
Constitution as published, including its punctuation.

## graph.json

- `groups`: group id to label (leyes-mx catalog).
- `nodes[]` (36): `id` (leyes-mx slug), `title`, `abbreviation`, `group`, `articles`,
  `words`, `published`, `lastReform`, `selfReferences`, `out` and `in` (degree).
- `edges[]`: `source` cites `target`; `mentions` (exact name mentions), `articles`
  (citations to articles that exist in the target), `top` (most cited articles, `[id,
  count]`).
- `constitution`: constitutional article id to citing law to count.
- `stats`: totals for mentions, article citations (resolved and unresolved), "artículo
  N constitucional" citations and names skipped as regulations.
